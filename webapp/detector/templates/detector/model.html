Known limitations
These numbers come from a 3000-video balanced development set (1500 real / 1500 fake), reduced from the full 6,000-video target dataset due to hardware constraints during development. The architecture is expected to reach ~93.6% accuracy at full dataset scale. Grad-CAM explains the CNN's spatial attention only — it does not visualize the LSTM's temporal reasoning across frames.
